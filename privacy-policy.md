# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 28, 2026**

## Overview
Sosmed Scheduler is a personal productivity tool 
that allows users to schedule and publish social media content 
to their connected accounts, including TikTok.

## Data We Collect
- TikTok account information (username, profile picture) 
  obtained via Login Kit
- Content you choose to schedule or publish through the App

## How We Use Your Data
- To authenticate your TikTok account
- To publish scheduled content on your behalf

## Data Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.
